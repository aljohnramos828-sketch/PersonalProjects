# Indian Retail Sales Performance Analysis Using SQL and Tableau

## Overview

This project analyzes an Indian retail sales dataset using MySQL to explore sales performance, product profitability, regional differences, and customer satisfaction.

The goal is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights that can help management understand which products perform best, which regions have stronger sales, and how customer satisfaction relates to sales performance.

## Objectives

- Evaluate the profitability of different product categories.
- Compare average sales across regions and cities.
- Rank products by net sales within their respective categories.
- Identify the top 10 best-selling products based on quantity sold.
- Examine average net sales across customer satisfaction levels and sales channels.

## Tools and Technologies

- MySQL
- SQL aggregation functions
- GROUP BY and ORDER BY
- Window functions and product ranking
- Business metrics, including revenue, profit, quantity sold, and profit margin

## Analysis Performed

1. Customer Satisfaction and Channel Analysis
2. Product Category Profitability Analysis
3. Regional and City Sales Analysis
4. Top 10 Best-Selling Products

## Key Findings

1. Product Performance
The Top 10 Best-Selling Products visualization highlights the products with the highest sales quantities. These products can help identify popular items and inform inventory planning.
2. Category Profitability
The Profit per Category chart enables comparisons of profit contributions across product categories, helping management identify which categories generate the most profit.
3. Geographic Profitability
The Profit per City, supports comparisons of profitability across locations and can help identify stronger and weaker markets.
4. Customer Satisfaction and Sales Channels
The average net sales by customer satisfaction level and channel visualization allows the business to examine whether sales performance differs across customer satisfaction ratings and sales channels.
